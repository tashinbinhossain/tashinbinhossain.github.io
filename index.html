01 / ABOUT

Curious about the
systems behind biology.

My background is in biology, and I’m learning to use computational approaches to investigate biological questions.

I completed my M.Sc. in Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Bangladesh. My thesis investigated the anti-arthritic potential of Kalanchoe pinnata, combining network pharmacology and molecular docking with qRT-PCR measurement of IL-6 in a mouse arthritis model.

Alongside it, I work on computational projects: single-cell RNA-seq analysis of lupus PBMCs, a network-proximity pipeline for drug repurposing in rheumatoid arthritis (in progress), and graph-based gene–disease prediction.

I’m looking for research environments where experimental biology, data analysis, and careful interpretation inform one another.

Single-Cell RNA-seq Analysis of SLE PBMC Data

Built a Scanpy/AnnData workflow for pediatric and adult SLE and healthy-donor datasets, annotating 12 major PBMC populations across approximately 33,600 cells. Also compared PCA and scGPT zero-shot embeddings; cell labels come from PCA-based marker annotation, so purity scores favor PCA.
